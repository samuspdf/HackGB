# Highline Warren – Milwaukee (automotive chemicals; parent Highline Warren LLC)
TRI ID 53209RXFRD3100W · 3100 W Mill Rd · permits: CAA, RCRA hazardous-waste generator, TRI. No water discharge permit on file.

**What it releases (2024, on-site 12,974 lb, all air):** methanol 12,974 (8,558 fugitive, 4,416 stack). Ethylene glycol and certain glycol ethers were reported but with 0 lb released. **Off-site:** 19,519 lb of methanol sent for treatment.

**Trend:** methanol releases rose from 7,790 lb (2020) to 12,974 lb (2024).

**Background:** Highline Warren formed in 2020 when Pritzker Private Capital combined Highline Aftermarket, the largest US maker of windshield washer fluid and other automotive chemicals, with Warren Distribution. Methanol is the main ingredient of washer fluid. That this site blends washer fluid is likely but not confirmed.

**Where it goes / what it touches**
- **Air:** methanol, mostly escaping from leaks and vents, reaches the surrounding neighborhood on Milwaukee's northwest side. Methanol is an ozone-forming VOC, so the plant is a **possible contributor** to Milwaukee County's ozone problem.
- **Water:** about 1.1 miles from the Milwaukee River and 1.2 miles from Lincoln Creek (USGS hydrography). No reported releases to water.
- **People within 1 mile:** 10,985 residents. 66% people of color (the highest share of any factory on this map), 37% low-income, 11% under age 5 (the highest share on this map), 13% seniors.
- **Schools within 1 mile (5):** Bader Hillel High School, Glen Hills Middle, HOPE Christian School: Fidelis, Hawthorne Elementary, Thurston Woods Elementary. 52 within 3 miles.

**Compliance record (separate from releases)**
- Hazardous waste (RCRA): inspected 12/17/2025; **Notice of Noncompliance 01/04/2026** (state). Flagged in 2 recent quarters.
- Air (CAA): 1 inspection, 10/18/2022. No violations.
- Formal enforcement actions and penalties: none on record.
