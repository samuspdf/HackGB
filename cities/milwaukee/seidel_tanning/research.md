# Seidel Tanning Corp (leather tannery; family-owned, founded 1945)
TRI ID 53212SDLTN1306E · 1306 E Meinecke Ave · permits: Clean Air Act (CAA) operating permit, TRI. No water discharge permit on file.

**What it releases (2024, on-site 32,573 lb, all air)**

| Chemical | Air (lb) | Off-site disposal (lb) | To sewer plant (lb) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Certain glycol ethers | 29,662 (stack) | — | — | Finishing solvents; VOC |
| Formic acid | 2,908 (fugitive) | — | — | Used in tanning; sharp, irritating vapor |
| Chromium compounds | 3.1 (stack) | 56,494.6 | 333.6 | Trivalent chromium from chrome tanning; not flagged as a carcinogen in TRI |

**Trend:** on-site releases rose from 19,507 lb (2020) to 32,573 lb (2024), mostly glycol ethers (18,189 → 29,662 lb). Chromium sent off-site rose from about 21,200 lb (2020) to about 56,500 lb (2024).

**Background:** founded by Irwin Seidel in 1945 in the old Pfister & Vogel tanning district along the Milwaukee River. It's described as the only Milwaukee tannery operating continuously since World War II, and one of the largest US producers of leather for footwear.

**Where it goes / what it touches**
- **Air:** glycol ethers and formic acid reach the surrounding neighborhood (Riverwest, with the East Side across the river). Both are ozone-forming VOCs, so the tannery is a **possible contributor** to Milwaukee County's ozone problem (2015 ozone standard nonattainment).
- **Water:** the plant is about 0.1 mile from the Milwaukee River (USGS hydrography), but it reports no releases to water and has no water discharge permit. Its sewer transfers (chromium, 334 lb) go to the Milwaukee Metropolitan Sewerage District (MMSD) for treatment.
- **Waste:** about 56,500 lb of chromium compounds went off-site for disposal in 2024.
- **People within 1 mile:** 38,177 residents. 33% people of color, 39% low-income, 3% under age 5, 13% seniors (ECHO, ACS 2024).
- **Schools within 1 mile (12):** Clara Mohammed School, Carver Academy, Cass Street Elementary, Gaenslen Elementary, Holmes Elementary, King International Baccalaureate Middle, Maryland Montessori, Riverside High, Riverwest Elementary, St. Marcus Lutheran School, St. Philip's Lutheran School, Tamarack Waldorf School. 73 schools within 3 miles.

**Compliance record (separate from releases)**
- Air (CAA): 2 inspections, last 12/18/2024. No violations. An inspection-related action on 10/08/2025 closed with no further action.
- Hazardous waste (RCRA): inactive handler, no violations.
- Formal enforcement actions and penalties: none on record.
