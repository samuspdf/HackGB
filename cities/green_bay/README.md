# Green Bay (Brown County)

**Local setting:** The Lower Fox River flows north through the city into the bay of Green Bay, and the East River joins it downtown. Both lie inside the **Lower Green Bay & Fox River Area of Concern**, home of one of the largest PCB sediment cleanups ever (more than 6 million cubic yards dredged over 39 miles, about $1 billion, 2004–2020). The Fox River is on the 303(d) impaired list for **nutrients (phosphorus), PCBs and turbidity**, and it doesn't meet its fish-consumption, recreation or aquatic-life uses. About 1.1–1.2 million lb of phosphorus a year flows from the Fox into Green Bay (UW-Extension and a 2014 NEW Water estimate), mostly from farm and urban runoff. Bay Beach was closed to swimming in the late 1930s–1940s because of pollution, and the city has been working to reopen it. Brown County is not in an ozone nonattainment area.
